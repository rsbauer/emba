## Problem 1

Consider the market price for t-shirts in Tourist Town, USA is described by the following demand and supply equations: QD and QS below:

Quantity demanded = 24000 - 500P 
Quantity supplied = -6000 + 1000P

For market equillibrium, quantity demanded = quantity supplied

Isolate P to get price

24000 - 500P = -6000 + 1000P 
24000 + 6000 = 1500P
30000 = 1500P 
P = 20


## The cheats

if asking for market price, you have to solve for P.  Once you know the price, you can solve for quantity by plugging in the price.
