# Casing Case Studies
## What is a case study?

A summary of real life business scenario/challenge

Grounded in org data, interviews, and context

## An efficient case study

Is better than an essay
Surface multiple view points
Facilitates multi-dimensional thinking
Provide avenues for connecting theory and framework to a live practical application

> The case does not hand you the answer. It hands you the ambiguity a real leader had to work through


# Why case studies

## For students 
decision-making in complex situations 
improves ability to reason and analyze across situations and disciplines
Peer learning environment

## For instructors
Balance inductive vs deductive reasoning
Foster student engagement
Multifaceted approach to employing cases

The question: Should RMHC evolve beyond its historic focus on housing families near hospitals, and if so, how - across a federated system of roughly 250 independently governed chapters worldwide?

## Theory of Change (framework) - old vs proposed


|                  | Inputs/Activities                            | Outputs                                                                 | Outcomes                                                               | Impact                                                   |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Historic ToC** | Build houses and family rooms near hospitcal | nights of lodging provided                                              | reduce family burden during hospital stay                              | families stay closer, together for less                  |
| **Proposed ToC** | Support before, during, and after treatment  | coordinate pre/post care, referrals financial and mental health support | reduce financial distress and caregiver strain across teh full journey | improved family well-being and health outcomes over time |

> A theory of change should evolve when evidence shows a gap between current activities and the outcomes the mission requires
