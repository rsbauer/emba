## Summary

### 💡 Course Focus & Objectives
The class focuses on the **strategic and economic use of information systems and data** to gain a competitive advantage. Key areas of study include:
*   Analyzing new business models and market trends.
*   Understanding the value of networks, platform effects, and data.
*   Identifying disruptive technologies and ethical responsibilities.

### ⚙️ Defining Information Systems (IS)
The core concept is that an Information System is not just technology. An IS encompasses four elements:
1.  **Technical** components (hardware/software).
2.  **Process** requirements.
3.  **People** (users and staff).
4.  **Structure** (organizational framework).

*   **Data Flow:** The progression of value is from **Data $\rightarrow$ Information $\rightarrow$ Knowledge**.
*   **Purpose at Work:** IS are used to improve efficiency, reduce costs, meet regulatory needs, or help the organization stay competitive.

### 💼 Project & Practical Application
*   **Goal:** Students must identify a real-world business problem (preferably data-related) and propose an AI/tech solution.
*   **Deliverable:** The project requires a clear **business case**, detailing how the process works today versus what is proposed.
*   **Format:** Teams of 2–4 people will work on the project, which may involve building solutions like websites or chatbots.
