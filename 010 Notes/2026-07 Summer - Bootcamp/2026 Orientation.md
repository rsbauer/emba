**Zicklin Executive Orientation 2026  
The Louis V. Gerstner, Jr. Center for Learning  
(IBM Learning Center)  
Armonk, NY  
August 21-22, 2026**  
  
**All new Executive students are required to attend the orientation program.** If you have any questions or concerns, please email us at zicklinexecutivestudents@baruch.cuny.edu.  
  
Transportation is offered to/from Baruch College (55 Lexington Ave, New York, NY) and the orientation site (Armonk, NY) for students, faculty, and staff.
